Our Story

Bahari means “of the sea,” and our flavours carry that spirit — vibrant, layered, and full of life. Born from a love of homecooked Indian food and coastal traditions, Bahari Flavours celebrates heritage through taste. We honour the recipes passed down through generations while crafting products that fit beautifully into modern kitchens.

How to enjoy Bahari Flavours?

 Cook: Add depth to curries, dals, and stirfries.  Snack: Enjoy our savoury bites anytime.  Dip: Pair chutneys with meals, snacks, or sandwiches.  Elevate: Turn simple dishes into flavourful favourites.

Community & Values

We believe food is connection — to land, to memory, and to one another. Bahari Flavours is committed to ethical sourcing, cultural respect, and celebrating the diverse culinary traditions that shape our world.
